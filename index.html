Shared fruit · Solo replay · Build a player and enter

Two players search the same round at once, taking turns at one backward expansion each from the point A, 300 turns apiece. A level-9 cell belongs to whoever reaches it first, so going where the rival has already been wins nothing. Each pairing plays every round twice with the seats swapped. The cloud is the round's filled Bristorian J set (12 steps), and every mark is a cell, coloured by its first claimant.

Test a weights file (or drop one here) against the pool on the 16 public rounds. It runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Your player · practice

Float tallies on the public rounds, 32 games per pairing. Official scores come from the exact referee on hidden rounds after you enter.

Reading it. Orange squares are cells the shared policy claimed first; blue rings are cells its opponent claimed first. The replay follows the real turn clock: claims appear on the turn that made them. "Landed on taken cells" counts leaves that arrived after the cell was already owned, which is wasted budget. Drag to turn the cloud. Space plays or pauses, and the arrow keys step games.

What is exact. The scores are the referee's: referee_shared.py checks every leaf in dyadic integer arithmetic, refuses logs out of turn order, and re-derives ownership from the log. In every game, the marked ownership equals the referee's; shared_data.mjs refuses to build otherwise. The referee does not certify the budget or which search produced a leaf; the arena runs both searches and writes the log. The cloud is a finite float32 GPU preview for orientation, and the marks are drawn over it without depth occlusion.

Custody. The hidden seeds were committed by hash before any shared-fruit code ran (SHARED_COMMITMENT.md), along with the acceptance rule. The entry was frozen by SHA-256 before the seeds were opened. The same seat trained the entry and held the nonce: procedural custody. The shared policy is 121 int8 weights that see one public fact about rivals, where they have expanded.
