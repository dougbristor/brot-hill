Brot Hill · match replay

Shared fruit · Solo replay · Build a player and enter

Each hill round is a point A and a constant c. Two entrants search backward from A under Fc(w) = B(w) + c on the same 300-expansion budget, and each scores the distinct ε = 1/16 cells its level-9 leaves land in. The cloud is the round's filled Bristorian J set (12 steps). The marks show which entrant claimed each cell.

Reading it. Orange squares are cells only the little policy reached, blue rings are cells only the opponent reached, and pale diamonds are cells both reached. The replay reveals each entrant's cells in the order its leaves came out of the search, paced by leaf count rather than by budget unit. Drag to turn the cloud. Space plays or pauses, and the arrow keys step rounds.

What is exact. The scores are the referee's: referee.py checks every leaf in dyadic integer arithmetic and shares no code with the arena. On every round, the marked cells equal the referee's cell count; match_data.mjs refuses to build otherwise. The cloud is a finite 12-step GPU preview in float32, for orientation only, and the marks are drawn over it without depth occlusion.

Custody. The hill seeds were committed by hash before training (HILL_COMMITMENT.md), and the entry was frozen by SHA-256 before they were opened. The same seat trained the entry and held the nonce, so this is procedural custody. The little policy is 109 int8 weights, not a language model, and these 32 rounds say nothing beyond these rules.
